## 完整提示词

Use case: logo-brand. Create a polished original character concept sheet for Leaf, a minimal desktop PDF reader. Synthesize BOTH supplied reference images into ONE consistent adorable black cat mascot.
Reference 1 supplies the intellectual character cues: oversized round reading spectacles, an open book in one paw, a short pencil in the other, a sweet slightly earnest bookish attitude. Do NOT use its photorealism, orange fur, jewelry, or detailed textures.
Reference 2 supplies the graphic language: a solid near-black silhouette, upright triangular cat ears, huge light eyes with simple black pupils, tiny nose, sparse whiskers, extremely minimal hand-drawn flat shapes. Evolve the proportions into a distinctive original compact character: a large slightly wide head, gently softened triangular ears, tiny rounded body, short paws, and a curling expressive tail.
Key design challenge: make the round spectacles unmistakably read as glasses on a black cat, NOT merely two white circular eyes. Use two fine muted sage-green circular frames with a clear connecting bridge and short temple arms extending toward the sides of the head; INSIDE each lens is a separate smaller warm-white oval eye and dark dot pupil, with a little dark space between eye and frame. Thin green frames remain visible against the black face. Consistent glasses in every pose, in the sleeping pose glasses can be pushed up onto forehead.
Flat ink-black body, warm-white eye shapes and book pages, restrained muted sage-green glasses and book cover, small warm ochre pencil. Two or three short black whisker strokes each side, no fur detail. No clothing or necklace, no decorative badges or extra symbols. Slight hand-drawn irregularity, clean confident outlines, no rendering, no 3D, no shading, no gradients, no paper grain. Cute, quiet, inquisitive, gently funny, sophisticated enough for a modern reading app.
Landscape white design sheet with generous whitespace. Left side: ONE large full-body main pose, facing viewer, holding an open sage-green book in one paw and a short ochre pencil in the other. Book is simple with only a couple short lines on the pages, face unobstructed. Right side: three small separate consistent character poses stacked vertically: reading intently with book held in both paws; peeking over a book and adjusting spectacles; curled up asleep against a closed book with spectacles pushed up. Same recognizable character and palette in all four drawings. All drawings fully in frame and separated. A small handwritten 'Leaf' at upper left only. No other text, no UI, no mockup, no labels, no layout borders, no watermark. Focus on a strong simple graphic identity combining a black doodle cat with a round-spectacled reader.
