## 生成提示词

Use case: logo-brand. Create an original mascot character design sheet for Leaf, a minimal modern desktop PDF reading app. The mascot is a very cute, quiet, slightly curious little CAT rendered as extremely simple hand-drawn line art. Premium restrained editorial doodle, approachable and warm, not a busy children's illustration. Consistent character: round squishy head flowing into a small pear-shaped body, two small recognizable cat ears, tiny widely spaced dot eyes, a tiny understated w mouth, very short rounded paws, one smoothly curled tail. Signature identifying feature: one ear has a small sage-green leaf-shaped inner patch; the ear remains recognizably a cat ear, no sprout on the head. Warm ivory body, near-black charcoal outlines with uniform medium weight, round caps, subtly imperfect organic lines, flat muted sage green only on the ear patch and simple book cover. No shading, no gradients, no texture, no fur strokes, no clothing, no accessories, no pink blush. Make the silhouette readable at small UI sizes with very few strokes. Design sheet on a pure white background, beautifully composed generous whitespace, landscape format. Left 55 percent: ONE large main mascot sitting upright and holding an open small green book, face completely visible above book, friendly quiet expression, complete tail visible. Right 45 percent: three smaller well-separated consistent poses arranged vertically: cat peeking over a closed book, cat curled up asleep against a book, cat sitting with one tiny paw raised in greeting. These are the exact same character with identical defining features and proportions, not alternative designs. One small tasteful typographic label 'Leaf' in upper left; no other text, no layout boxes, no UI screenshots, no mockups, no watermarks. Actual deliverable is a polished original simple cat mascot exploration sheet for brand review, with clear large character drawings.
